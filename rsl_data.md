# RAID Starter Account Progression Guide

Oct 17, 2026 · @Marvin

Build 8 champions to Rank 6 on Day 1 (Gizmak, Galleus, Maneater, Pain Keeper, Deacon Armstrong, Ninja, Artak, Rathalos), then 5 more over the next weeks. These 13 cover every content area you listed. Your real bottlenecks are Speed gear, ascension potions and masteries, not XP or energy.

**Why these, in priority order** \[Sourced ratings → My call\]:

- **Ninja is the Clan Boss anchor.** HellHades rates him 10/10/10 for Demon Lord/Hydra/Chimera, Ayumilove 5/5/5 ([HH Ninja](https://hellhades.com/raid/champions/ninja/), [Ayumilove Ninja](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-ninja-skill-mastery-equip-guide/)). He has a dedicated speed slot (161–165) in the Budget Maneater tune.
- **Maneater + Pain Keeper are the unkillable engine.** Maneater's A3 gives the team Unkillable + Block Debuffs; Pain Keeper's A3 cuts all ally cooldowns by 1 ([HH Maneater](https://hellhades.com/champion-spotlight-maneater/), [Ayumilove Pain Keeper](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-pain-keeper-skill-mastery-equip-guide/)). Both are HH Clan Boss 10; Maneater is also Spider 10.
- **Rathalos is the second Clan Boss DPS and a Spider/Hydra carry.** His A1 Decrease DEF is unresistable vs bosses, his A2 ignores 100% boss DEF, and he deals +50% vs burned targets ([Ayumilove Rathalos](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-rathalos-blademaster-skill-mastery-equip-guide/)). HH: CB 9 / Hydra 10 / Spider 10 / Dragon 9.
- **Artak is the multi-dungeon burner.** HH Hydra/Spider/Dragon/IG 10, CB 9 ([HH Artak](https://hellhades.com/raid/champions/artak/)). AoE HP Burn, burn activation and AoE Decrease ATK; HellHades calls him a top Ice Golem Hard pick ([HH HP Burn guide](https://hellhades.com/best-hp-burn-champions-in-raid-shadow-legends-complete-guide/)).
- **Deacon is the #1 dungeon all-rounder.** Ayumilove 5★ in all four dungeons plus Clan Boss and Arena; HH CB 10 / Spider 10 ([Ayumilove Deacon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-deacon-armstrong-skill-mastery-equip-guide/)).
- **Rathalos before Kymar** \[My call\]: Clan Boss is your #1 priority. Weighted by your priorities, Rathalos scores 8.98 vs Kymar 8.05 on HH ratings. Kymar is only HH CB 5 but Spider/Dragon/IG/Arena 10 ([HH Kymar](https://hellhades.com/raid/champions/prince-kymar/)), so he is the first Wave-2 build.
- **Ruella or Criodan for Fire Knight** \[My call\]: Ruella is HH FK 10 / CB 8 / Chimera 8 and on HellHades' best Chimera epics list ([HH Chimera epics](https://hellhades.com/10-great-epic-champions-for-the-chimera/)). Criodan the Blue (HH FK 8) is better if you later push Fire Knight Hard, where Freeze matters ([HH FK Hard](https://hellhades.com/understanding-fire-knight-hard-mode-mechanics-in-raid-shadow-legends/)), and in Spider/IG. Pick Ruella for Chimera overlap, Criodan for FK Hard.
- **Your Mythicals carry but don't enable unkillable.** Neither gives team-wide Unkillable (both only self). Gizmak's team +30% SPD + Extra Turn and Galleus' Turn Meter steal + Extra Turn break Maneater tune timing ([Ayumilove Galleus](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-galleus-bloodcrest-skill-mastery-equip-guide/), [Ayumilove Gizmak](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-gizmak-the-terrible-skill-mastery-equip-guide/)). They carry you before the tune works, and afterwards carry Hydra, Chimera, Spider, Dragon, Ice Golem and Arena. HellHades' Nub calls Gizmak the most underrated Mythical ([HH Nub Mythical list](https://hellhades.com/nubs-mythical-tier-list-raid-shadow-legends/)); Galleus got a solid buff in patch 10.45 (May 2025).

**Deliberately not built early** \[My call\]: Skeletor (HH 6, weakest Legendary here), Chronicler Adelyn (a Sand Devil specialist, though Ayumilove rates her Chimera 5/5), Aleksandr, Leminisi, Loki (CB 4), Sun Wukong (CB 5), Alice, Michelangelo, Ezio, Xenomorph, Dorothy, Graal and Visix. Several are good (Michelangelo, Dorothy and Ezio are HH 9), but each would be a 14th+ build. **Loki** (Hydra Block Buffs) and **Graal** (Spider) are the first luxury adds once the 13 are done.

## 2. Phased roadmap

Day 1 builds the 8; Weeks 1–4 farm Speed gear and climb Clan Boss to Brutal; Month 2+ pushes UNM, stage 25 dungeons, Hydra and Chimera.

### Day 1 checklist (in this order)

- [ ] **Join an active clan that already kills high-tier Demon Lord.** Demon Lord, Hydra and Chimera all need a clan, and difficulty tiers unlock clan-wide ([Plarium Demon Lord guide](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014645900-Guide-Demon-Lord)).
- [ ] **XP Barrel on Gizmak.** He starts at Rank 6, Level 1; the Barrel maxes a champion's level for its current rank, worth about 2.0M XP on a fresh R6 ([Plarium Tavern](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014648380-The-Tavern)).
- [ ] **Brew Galleus to 60 with about 108 Force brews.** He is Force affinity, so each brew gives 18,750 XP. Use the Instant Brew Upgrade screen with its affinity filter ([Plarium Brew Upgrade](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22806729480348-Guide-Brew-Upgrade)).
- [ ] **Buy the Gem Mine (3 × 500 = 1,500 gems).** 15 gems/day, pays back in about 100 days ([HH Gem Mine](https://hellhades.com/raid-shadow-legends-is-the-gem-mine-worth-it-in-2022/)).
- [ ] **Feasts:** brew Maneater and Deacon to their rank cap (Lv40 if 4★) and Pain Keeper to hers (Lv30 if 3★). Use one Feast on each (→ R6 L1), then brew all three to 60.
- [ ] **Chickens:** brew Ninja, Artak and Rathalos to Lv50, rank each up with 5 × 5★ chickens (15 of your 16), then brew to 60.
- [ ] **Tomes:** Legendary → Ninja A3 ×4, Ninja A2 ×5. Epic → Maneater A3 ×2, Deacon A3 ×2, then finish both. Rare → Pain Keeper A3 ×3 first, then all of her (15). Details in section 6.
- [ ] **Instant masteries (800 gems each) on Gizmak and Ninja** — your carry/farmer and your Clan Boss damage ([HH gems vs Minotaur](https://hellhades.com/gems-vs-farming-minotaurs-labyrinth/)).
- [ ] **Gear:** press Auto-Equip (added in 11.50) for a baseline ([Ayumilove 2026 patches](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2026/)), then hand-place Speed boots, C.RATE gloves and ACC chests on supports and debuffers ([HH Nub tips](https://hellhades.com/nubs-top-50-tips-for-new-players-in-raid-shadow-legends/)).
- [ ] **Start daily loops:** Clan Boss with the starter team (section 3), Classic Arena for medals, campaign push with Galleus (Alt form clears waves) and Gizmak to raise account level.

### Week 1

- **Skullcrusher to 5★ Lv50** as the stun target: 4 × 4★ chickens plus about 76 matched brews; 1 Epic tome on his A2 (CD 4→3) ([HH Skullcrusher](https://hellhades.com/raid/champions/skullcrusher/)).
- **Potion Keeps:** ascend Deacon to Asc 3 first (his A3 gains an Extra Turn) ([HH ascension](https://hellhades.com/raid/guide-to-champion-ascension/)), then Pain Keeper (Lesser/Greater potions only), Maneater, Ninja.
- **Dragon:** climb with Maneater, Deacon, Ninja, Rathalos, Galleus. Farm Speed sets above everything else; HellHades calls Speed gear critical on a fresh account ([HH Project Zero](https://hellhades.com/project-zero-my-first-two-days-on-a-fresh-raid-account/)).
- **Minotaur:** climb toward stage 15 for mastery scrolls (Maneater, Pain Keeper, Rathalos, Deacon, Artak).
- **Spider 10:** accessories and silver.
- **Clan Boss:** push the starter team to Brutal — books and shards only drop from Brutal up ([DWJ first CB team](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=3542)).

### Weeks 2–4

- **Gear the Budget Maneater tune:** Maneater 240–241, Pain Keeper 218–222, Ninja 161–165, Rathalos 175–178, Skullcrusher 111–118. Check every build in the DeadwoodJedi calculator, then switch to the tune on Brutal.
- **Build Wave 2 in order:** Kymar → Ultimate Deathknight → Seer → Ruella → Skullcrusher 6★ (math in section 5).
- **Dungeons:** Spider and Dragon toward stage 20 (the efficiency sweet spot); start Fire Knight and Ice Golem at 10+.
- **Unlocks:** account level 33 → Chimera on Easy; 35 → Tag Team Arena and Advanced Quests; 40 → Doom Tower and +16 artifacts ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/raid-shadow-legends-what-to-do-after-level-20-on-an-alt-account/)).

### Month 2+

- **Clan Boss:** Budget Maneater on Nightmare (author expects 2 keys), then Ultra-Nightmare (the tune page claims 2 keys; the build article says 3–4) ([DWJ New Budget Unkillable](https://deadwoodjedi.com/new-budget-unkillable/)).
- **Dungeons:** Spider and Dragon to 25, then Fire Knight and Ice Golem to 20–25. Hard mode opens after 25 and drops Mythical gear from Hard 26+ ([InTeleria drop rates](https://www.inteleria.com/raid-dungeon-drop-rates/)).
- **Hydra (level 45):** start on Normal ([BlueStacks Hydra](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/raid-shadow-legends/rsl-hydra-guide-en.html)).
- **Iron Twins:** farm Soul Coins to awaken champions for blessings ([InTeleria blessings](https://www.inteleria.com/raid-shadow-legends-blessings-guide/)).
- **Faction Wars:** start once you have levelled champions in several factions; it becomes the main gear and Perception-set source after month one.
- **Arena:** push Classic to Gold V for Arbiter missions.

## 3. Clan Boss: Demon Lord, Hydra, Chimera (priority #1)

Run a free starter team now, then switch to DeadwoodJedi's **Budget Maneater Unkillable (Ninja variant)** — the only documented unkillable tune your roster can build, rated 2 keys on Ultra-Nightmare when fully geared ([DWJ](https://deadwoodjedi.com/speed-tunes/budget-maneater-unkillable-ninja/)).

### Demon Lord rules that shape everything \[Sourced\]

- Immune to Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Decrease SPD, Provoke and Fear. Only **one** HP Burn counts at a time. From boss turn 50 it ignores Unkillable and Block Damage.

Decrease ATK is useless once you are unkillable ([DWJ](https://deadwoodjedi.com/clan-boss-its-easy-if-you-know-how/)).

### Your 133 Clan Boss keys \[Sourced mechanics → My call plan\]

Rewards are **one set of chests per day, based on that day's total damage**, so dumping all 133 keys in one day earns a single day's chest ([Plarium DL](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014645900-Guide-Demon-Lord)). DWJ's top-chest thresholds (from 2022): Easy 1.11M, Normal 3.63M, Hard 11.64M, Brutal 21.69M, NM 39.16M, UNM 70.27M ([DWJ top chest](https://deadwoodjedi.com/how-to-get-top-chest-in-clan-boss/)).

- **Each day:** use your free keys plus just enough stored keys to cross the next chest threshold at the highest difficulty you survive. Keys needed = threshold ÷ damage per key, minus free keys.
- **Example:** a starter team doing \~5M per Brutal key needs \~5 keys for Brutal top chest → 2–3 stored keys per day.
- **Pacing:** 133 keys last roughly 30–60 days of bonus hits, covering the whole Brutal → NM climb when extra chests matter most.
- **Don't waste regen:** you hold at most 2 natural keys, refreshed every 6 hours.

### Hydra (level 45; 3 keys/week; 6 champions per key, each once per week) \[Sourced mechanics; team My call\]

Head counters: **Decay** → Provoke · **Torment** → Perfect Veil / fear cleanse · **Mischief / Suffering / Blight** → Block Buffs (HP Burn also cancels Blight's Poison Cloud) · **Wrath** → Decrease ATK, shields, revives ([HH getting started](https://hellhades.com/getting-started-with-hydra-clan-boss-in-raid-shadow-legends/), [HH Block Buffs](https://hellhades.com/best-block-buffs-champions-for-hydra-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). HP Burn is capped at 20k per tick; Blight takes double burn damage ([Ayumilove Hydra](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)).

| Key | Team | Roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Galleus (Base), Gizmak, Ninja, Rathalos, Artak, Ultimate Deathknight | Galleus Provokes Decay and gives team Revive on Death; Gizmak's unresistable burn counters Blight; Artak + UDK cover Decrease ATK and shields vs Wrath; Ninja + Rathalos deal damage |
| 2–3 | Maneater, Pain Keeper, Deacon, Kymar, Seer, Skullcrusher/Ruella | Whatever is built; accept lower chests |

- **Gap:** no roster champion gives team-wide Perfect Veil or a fear cleanse, so Torment is your weak head. **Loki** (Block Buffs, one-ally Veil, +60 ACC aura) is the best single Hydra add later.
- **Don't rely on Gizmak's Taunt** to stop digestion; the 2024 rework reportedly removed that.
- Chest floors: Normal \~1.67M, Hard \~5.1M ([Ayumilove Hydra](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)). Start on Normal.

### Chimera (level 33; 2 keys/week; 5 champions; up to 65 boss turns) \[Sourced mechanics; team My call\]

Forms cycle every 5 turns: Ultimate → Ram → Ultimate → Lion → Ultimate → Viper → Ultimate. **Ram** duels your lowest-C.DMG champion; **Lion** marks your highest-DEF champion, ignores Shield/Unkillable/Block Damage, but takes 5× Reflect damage; **Viper** stacks Necrosis per death, cleared by revives; **Ultimate** takes less damage while you keep buffs up ([Ayumilove Chimera](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-chimera-clan-boss-guide/), [BlueStacks Chimera](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/raid-shadow-legends/rsl-chimera-guide-en.html)).

| Slot | Champion | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Galleus (Base) | Likely Lion target (very high DEF); 100% boss reflect + self-heal; team Revive on Death clears Viper stacks |
| 2 | Gizmak | Team C.RATE/C.DMG/SPD buffs keep buffs up; free burn |
| 3 | Ninja | Ayumilove Chimera 5/5 |
| 4 | Rathalos | Chimera 4/5; unresistable Decrease DEF on bosses |
| 5 | Ultimate Deathknight (Wave 2) → Ruella | UDK: heal, shield, Decrease ATK, a deliberate low-C.DMG Duel target. Ruella: Decrease DEF / Weaken / Decrease SPD |

Chronicler Adelyn (Ayumilove Chimera 5/5) is the strongest unused Chimera option if you want a 14th build. Start on **Easy** (lowest milestone \~900K). Chimera is also the only source of the Mythical Embrys ([HH Chimera](https://hellhades.com/raid/boss-guide/chimera/)).

## 4. Dungeons and Arena

The same core runs all four dungeons: Deacon and Maneater appear in almost every team, Gizmak and Artak carry the burn teams, and Wave 2 (Kymar, Seer, UDK, Ruella, Skullcrusher) upgrades each one. Farm order: **Dragon (Speed) → Spider (accessories, silver) → Fire Knight (Savage, Regeneration) → Ice Golem (quests, Reflex)**.

Early stat targets by role, per HellHades (Oct 2026): damage dealers 170 SPD, 100% C.RATE, 150% C.DMG; debuffers 200 SPD, 200 ACC; buffers 200 SPD ([HH sets & stats](https://hellhades.com/best-sets-and-stat-combinations-for-every-champion-role-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). Stage 20 is the most efficient early farm (\~73% 5★ / 27% 6★ drops) ([InTeleria](https://www.inteleria.com/raid-dungeon-drop-rates/)).

### Spider's Den (priority #2)

**Mechanics** \[Sourced\]: the queen eats spiderlings to heal 3% and gain +10% ATK each. She is immune to Stun, Freeze, Provoke and Block Active Skills, but spiderlings can be controlled. Stage 20: Spirit boss, 200 ACC / 200 RES → you need **225 ACC** (250 safe). Stage 25: Magic boss, spiderlings at 155 SPD → everyone **faster than 155**. Stages 21–25 halve Turn Meter reduction. Keep AoE damage low: dead spiderlings waste burn ticks ([Ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/), [HH zero-to-hero Spider](https://hellhades.com/zero-to-hero-spider-build-we-need-silver/)).

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Burn | Gizmak (Base) | Gizmak | Unresistable AoE burn each round at 100% C.RATE; A2 detonates and extends | 100% C.RATE, SPD, HP/DEF (not ATK) |
| Burn | Artak | Artak | AoE burn, activation, Decrease ATK; Spider Hard 5★ | 175+ SPD, 225+ ACC |

Spider is the only accessory source and the best silver farm ([HH Spider guide](https://hellhades.com/raid/boss-guide/skavag-the-spider-queen/)). Farm stage 10 early, then 20+.

Counters: Decrease ATK/DEF, Weaken, burst, Block Debuffs. Stage 20 = Magic, stage 25 = Force ([Ayumilove Dragon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-dragons-lair/)).

| 4 | Galleus | **Seer** | Galleus: Force (edge vs the Magic stage-20 boss), unresistable Alt Decrease DEF, wave clear; Ayumilove Dragon Hard 5. Seer: waves, Weaken, team C.RATE |
| 5 | Rathalos (or Gizmak) | **Ultimate Deathknight** | UDK: AoE Decrease ATK, shield, heal; HH Dragon 10 |

Farm Dragon first for Speed, Accuracy and Lifesteal sets ([Ayumilove artifacts](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifact-and-accessory-guide/), [HH 2025 gear meta](https://hellhades.com/2025-gear-set-meta-in-raid-whats-still-worth-farming/)).

### Fire Knight's Castle (priority #4)

**Mechanics** \[Sourced\]: his shield needs **10 hits (stages 10–20) / 12 hits (21–25)** to break. Multi-hitters should act before debuffers. ## 5. The 6★ plan with the math

Your stock makes 13 Rank 6s in total: 2 Mythicals + 3 Feasts + 3 from 5★ chickens on Day 1, then 5 more from 4★ chickens over weeks 2–6.

**Rules** \[Sourced\]: a real champion at its rank's level cap must be the base — chickens are fodder only. Rank N→N+1 costs N fodder of rank N. Level caps: R3 = 30, R4 = 40, R5 = 50, R6 = 60. A Feast takes a max-level champion of any rank to R6 L1; the Barrel maxes the current rank ([Plarium Tavern](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014648380-The-Tavern)). Mythicals arrive at Rank 6 ([HH Mythical preview](https://hellhades.com/raid-update-preview-mythical-champions/)). XP from level 1 to cap: 3★ 200,681 · 4★ 449,082 · 5★ 963,806 · 6★ 2,010,669 ([Ayumilove leveling](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-leveling-guide/)).

Assumed: Epics are at 4★, Legendaries at 5★, Rares at 3★. **Check each champion's current rank in-game** — anything already higher shortens the path.

**Why Feasts go on the Epics and the Rare:** a Feast on a 4★ Epic skips 4 × 4★ + 5 × 5★ fodder; on a Legendary it only skips 5 × 5★. Legendaries are the cheapest chicken targets.

## 7. What not to do

- Don't treat brews as ascension material, and don't spend them on fodder bases while core champions still need XP.
- Don't put Gizmak, Deacon, Galleus, Seer, Apothecary or High Khatun in the Maneater tune; no Counterattack gear/masteries on Ninja or Pain Keeper; no Relentless anywhere in it.
- Don't dump the 133 keys in one day — you only get one day's chest.
- Don't spread Legendary tomes past cooldown levels, and don't use higher-rarity tomes on lower-rarity champions.
- Don't use Feasts on Legendaries or the XP Barrel on a low-rank champion.
- Don't 6★ Skeletor, Adelyn, Loki, Sun Wukong or other nice-to-have Legendaries before the 13 are done.
- Don't build damage on Spider burners: keep Gizmak, Artak and Kymar on ACC/SPD/HP so spiderlings don't die early.
- Don't farm Ice Golem for gear early.
- Don't spend gems on energy, silver or shards.
- Don't +16 off-set or badly rolled pieces.
- Don't sacrifice Epics from the keep list or any Rare.
